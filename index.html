03 — Why Insomniac

The reason.

Personal Statement

"Insomniac doesn't just throw parties. It transforms unconventional spaces into worlds — and invites you to live inside them."
Victor Mateo Balestra · 2026

What excites me most about Insomniac is that you don't just produce events — you build immersive experiences.

By transforming unconventional spaces — like a 1932 mattress factory in Hialeah into Factory Town — you blend a city's raw character with exceptional design and sound to create unforgettable, music-led environments in unexpected places. That philosophy isn't a tagline. It's a civic commitment.

Living abroad has exposed me to how different cities approach music and nightlife culture — and it's made me appreciate how intentionally Insomniac builds its worlds. I'm currently in Valencia, Spain working with an events company called DERALI, where we produce daytime "coffee parties" and dance events featuring afro house and melodic house in repurposed cafés and restaurants. When I first arrived in Valencia in September, I was craving a community of creatives and like-minded people. Finding DERALI showed me what was possible when you build a gathering point for interesting people who otherwise may never cross paths.

Since then, I've been infatuated with what goes on behind the scenes. I took the experience and skills I learned working in Michelin kitchens and applied that hospitality mindset to the events space — planning the small details like the volume curve throughout a venue, the lighting around the instagrammable mirrors, the smell that hits guests when they walk in, and even the clever individualisation of the free drink voucher.

Seeing Insomniac elevate that same obsessive philosophy of world-building — at varying scales from EDC to Factory Town — while keeping the culture authentic and the experience second to none, is exactly what makes me want to be part of the team.
